## What Does an IPTV Family Plan Actually Mean?

Here is the part that causes most of the confusion: a connection is about **simultaneous streaming**, not simply how many devices you own.

For example, a household might have a Smart TV, two phones, a tablet and a Firestick. That does not automatically mean five streams are being used. The important question is how many of those devices are playing video at the same moment.

TiviLive currently offers plans from **1 connection up to 5 connections**, while keeping the core feature set consistent across the connection levels.

That makes the buying decision fairly simple:

* **1 connection:** one active stream at a time
* **2 connections:** two active streams
* **3 connections:** three active streams
* **4 connections:** four active streams
* **5 connections:** five active streams

So for a family, the right IPTV family plan usually comes down to your busiest viewing moment. Sunday evening is a much better test than counting everyone at breakfast.

## How Much Internet Speed Do You Need?

An IPTV family plan can have five connections on paper, but the home network still has to carry the load.

TiviLive's current FAQ recommends around **16 Mbps for HD streaming and 25+ Mbps for 4K/UHD**, and says a stable wired connection can help with consistency.

That does not mean every five-stream household automatically needs exactly five times the advertised number. Actual bandwidth use changes with resolution, compression and what each stream is doing. Still, this is an important point when a family is trying to run several streams at once.

If one person is watching 4K while three others are streaming HD, the router suddenly becomes one of the most important members of the family.
